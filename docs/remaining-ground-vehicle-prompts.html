The ten remaining ground vehicles

Detailed generation prompts for a chunky, exaggerated arcade enemy collection inspired by Megabonk. Broad armor panels, oversized weapons, matte colors, and silhouettes that read from the helicopter camera.

How to use: “Copy complete prompt” combines the shared style, the selected vehicle, and the negative prompt. Enable the 3D requirements when sending the prompt to a modeling agent or 3D generator.

Shared wheels: Generate wheel-free bodies for the Technical, Rocket Buggy, and Mortar Crawler. Attach instances of scenes/enemies/shared/universal_wheel.tscn during assembly. Tracked units use enclosed running gear.

Shared style prompt included with every complete prompt
Create an original low-poly enemy vehicle for a colorful arcade helicopter
combat game, with a playful, exaggerated aesthetic inspired by Megabonk.

ART DIRECTION:
Chunky proportions, bold silhouette, broad flat surfaces, visible polygon
facets, simple construction, slightly toy-like military machinery.
Make the vehicle threatening but a little absurd through oversized weapons
and squat proportions.

Prioritize readability from an elevated gameplay camera. The roof layout,
weapon shape, and overall silhouette must identify the enemy at a distance.
Use a few large recognizable shapes instead of many small details.

GEOMETRY:
Genuinely low-poly geometry with flat shading. Use approximately 6–10 sides
for small cylinders. Use bevels sparingly, only where they improve the
silhouette. Armor panels should be thick, angular, and deliberately simple.
Use solid geometry for important features.

MATERIALS:
Matte painted armor, charcoal mechanical parts, dark opaque windows,
and one strong accent color. Broad color blocks with minimal surface noise.
Simple painted vents and panel divisions. Consistent material style across
the entire vehicle collection.

PRESENTATION:
One vehicle only, centered, fully visible, isolated against a plain light-gray
background. Front three-quarter view, viewed slightly from above, showing
the front, one side, roof, and weapon clearly. Soft neutral lighting.
No environment, base, text, labels, interface, or dramatic effects.
Negative prompt included with every complete prompt
Photorealistic, high-poly, dense bevels, smooth subdivision surfaces,
micro-details, realistic military simulation, intricate machinery,
individual bolts, individual track links, complex suspension,
scratched noisy textures, heavy weathering, rust everywhere,
chrome, glossy plastic, transparent glass, thin fragile parts,
tiny unreadable markings, text, logos, watermarks, crew,
background scenery, diorama base, smoke, fire, muzzle flashes,
motion blur, depth of field, cropped vehicle, multiple vehicles,
exploded view, disconnected floating parts, fused weapon and hull,
asymmetric accidental geometry, warped barrels, blocked muzzle openings.
3D delivery requirements optional when copying
Deliver a modular game asset with separate logical moving parts:
Hull, TurretBody, GunCradle, and Weapon.
For the Gatling Heavy, also separate the rotating BarrelCluster.
For launchers, separate the elevating Launcher from its mounting base.

Keep static decorative details joined to their relevant parent mesh.
Do not create one object per bolt, panel, or vent.

Place turret yaw pivots at the mounting-ring center, elevation pivots at
the physical gun hinge, and the rotary-gun pivot along the barrel axis.
Provide named muzzle/socket empties where supported.

Export GLB/glTF 2.0. Final Godot orientation: +Y up and -Z forward.
Use meters. Ground contact at Y = 0, with the root centered between
the running gear.

Suggested geometry targets:
Light vehicle body and weapon: approximately 1,500–3,000 triangles.
Heavy tracked vehicle: approximately 2,500–4,500 triangles.
These are modeling targets, excluding the existing shared wheels.

Prefer one shared color atlas/material for armor and mechanical parts.
Use a separate emissive material only where necessary.
No baked lighting or painted cast shadows.

For wheeled vehicles, export no wheel geometry.
Use existing universal-wheel instances during assembly.
Store new source assets under assets/Enemy/, preserving the capital E.

Raider Technical

technical_truck · 4 shared wheels · Gun truck
Create a low-poly desert raider pickup truck with a heavy machine gun
mounted in its open rear bed.

SILHOUETTE:
A short, chunky pickup with a distinct front hood, upright two-seat cab,
and open rectangular cargo bed. Slightly oversized cab and broad stance.
The front should feel blunt and stubborn, with a thick bumper, two simple
square headlights, and a dark rectangular grille.

BODY:
Dusty sand-yellow paint, charcoal bumpers and chassis, muted brick-red
recognition panels. Use a dark opaque windshield split into two broad panes.
Add one thick rollbar behind the cab and low armored bed sides.
Include a few large welded armor plates with deliberately simple shapes.

WEAPON:
A single oversized heavy machine gun on a pedestal in the rear bed.
Give it a thick rectangular receiver, one long faceted barrel, a broad
muzzle opening, and one chunky ammunition box attached to the side.
The weapon should clearly read as a machine gun rather than a tank cannon.
Keep the receiver, elevation cradle, and mounting pedestal visually distinct.

WHEEL COMPATIBILITY:
Four-wheel layout with two axles. Do not generate wheels, tires, spare tires,
or detailed suspension. Leave four generous open wheel positions suitable
for attaching an existing shared off-road wheel.

CHARACTER:
Fast improvised raider vehicle; rough, practical, slightly ridiculous.
Its defining shape is a pickup cab followed by an exposed bed-mounted gun.

Suggested chassis length: approximately 4.4 meters.
No driver, gunner, flags, cargo clutter, or loose ammunition.
Pickup cab + exposed bed-mounted gun
02

Rocket Buggy

rocket_buggy · 4 shared wheels · Mobile rockets
Create a low-poly lightweight rocket attack buggy.

SILHOUETTE:
A low wedge-shaped nose, compact open cockpit, thick angular roll cage,
and short rear engine deck. Wide stance and short overhangs.
Use only a few thick structural bars so the cage remains readable.
It should look lighter, lower, and more exposed than an armored pickup.

BODY:
Muted olive armor with charcoal framework, brick-red rocket pods,
and small mustard-yellow warning panels.
Use a broad sloped hood, two simple front lamps, and a compact opaque
dashboard area. Keep the cockpit simple and empty.

WEAPON:
Two oversized rectangular rocket pods mounted side by side above the
rear deck on one compact rotating cradle.
Each pod has three large recessed launch openings across its front face.
Use thick pod walls and dark openings. Keep the rocket pods visually
separate from the roll cage, with clearance for aiming.
The two pods should dominate the roof silhouette.

WHEEL COMPATIBILITY:
Four-wheel layout with two axles. Generate no tires, wheels, spare wheels,
or elaborate suspension. Leave open wheel positions with generous clearance
for an existing universal off-road wheel.

CHARACTER:
An unstable-looking little raider carrying far too much rocket firepower.
Keep the body compact and the weapon exaggerated.

Suggested chassis length: approximately 4.6 meters.
No exposed engine plumbing, thin cables, driver, smoke, or launched rockets.
Low cage + twin rocket pods

Mortar Crawler

mortar_crawler · 6 shared wheels · Mobile artillery
Create a low-poly six-wheeled armored mortar support vehicle.

SILHOUETTE:
A squat rectangular armored body with a short sloping front and a broad
rear weapon deck. Three wheel positions on each side.
The body should feel compact, heavy, and stable rather than fast.

BODY:
Muted olive-green armor, charcoal lower chassis, and mustard-yellow
recognition panels. Broad flat armor plates with clipped corners.
Small dark front vision slits, a simple front bumper, and a low roof hatch.
Keep the roof clear around the mortar mounting ring.

WEAPON:
Two short, thick, open-ended mortar tubes mounted side by side in one
elevating cradle. Pose them upward at approximately 55 degrees.
Make the tubes noticeably wider and shorter than conventional cannon barrels.
Use dark recessed bores, thick rims, and a chunky shared hinge assembly.
The paired upward-pointing tubes are the main recognition feature.

WHEEL COMPATIBILITY:
Six-wheel layout with three evenly spaced axles.
Do not generate wheels or tires. Leave six open wheel positions and enough
space between neighboring wheels for an existing universal wheel asset.
Do not convert this vehicle into a tracked tank.

CHARACTER:
A little armored bombardment platform carrying two oversized stovepipe-like
mortars. Heavy, simple, and immediately recognizable from above.

Suggested chassis length: approximately 5 meters.
No deployed stabilizers, crew, ammunition piles, firing effects, or trailer.
Six-wheel body + twin raised mortars

SAM Launcher

sam_launcher · Enclosed tracks · Air defense
Create a low-poly tracked surface-to-air missile launcher.

SILHOUETTE:
A long, low armored chassis supporting four large cylindrical missile
canisters arranged in a clear two-by-two cluster.
Add one compact radar dish behind and above the launcher.
The tube cluster and radar must remain recognizable from an elevated camera.

BODY:
Desaturated blue-gray armor, charcoal running gear, pale ivory canisters,
and restrained brick-red canister end markings.
Use a sloping front plate, broad upper deck, and thick side armor skirts.

RUNNING GEAR:
Tracked chassis with enclosed running gear. Most road wheels and upper
track sections are hidden behind solid armor skirts. Show a simple dark
lower tread band and a few broad tread divisions.
Do not model individual track links or exposed suspension.

WEAPON:
Four thick faceted cylindrical canisters, two across and two high,
held by one substantial elevating cradle. Pose them moderately upward.
Use recessed dark launch openings with thick pale rims.
Mount the cradle on a distinct rotating base.
Use a simple low-poly radar dish with a thick support, not a fragile mast.

CHARACTER:
Dedicated air-defense machinery with a tall, unmistakable tube-cluster
silhouette. More orderly and technical than the raider vehicles.

Suggested chassis length: approximately 6.4 meters.
No wheels, secondary cannon, exposed missiles in flight, or effects.
Four cylindrical canisters + radar

Siege Tank

siege_tank · Enclosed tracks · Heavy cannon
Create a low-poly heavy siege tank built around one enormous artillery cannon.

SILHOUETTE:
Very broad, low hull; thick armored shoulders; large angular turret;
one exceptionally long and thick cannon.
The cannon should be the strongest visual feature, with a substantial
rectangular muzzle brake and a heavy armored mantlet.

BODY:
Dusty ochre-brown armor, charcoal tracks and weapon mechanics,
and small warm ivory recognition panels.
Use large sloped frontal plates and thick side skirts.
Give the turret a broad rear counterweight to balance the giant cannon.
Include one simple hatch and two broad rear engine vents.

RUNNING GEAR:
Enclosed tracked chassis. Hide road wheels behind deep armor skirts.
Only a narrow dark tread band should remain visible along the bottom.
Use simple continuous track forms rather than individual links.

WEAPON:
One heavy conventional cannon with an obvious round bore.
Thick faceted barrel, chunky recoil sleeve, and oversized muzzle brake.
Keep turret shell, elevation cradle, and recoiling barrel distinct.
The barrel should feel heavy and slightly absurd without becoming so long
that it overwhelms the entire vehicle.

CHARACTER:
Slow, stubborn siege machinery. This is the heaviest conventional tank
in the collection, with a strong low-and-wide silhouette.

Suggested hull length: approximately 7 meters, excluding cannon overhang.
No transport cabin, passenger windows, exposed tires, missile racks,
secondary turret, or deployed supports.
Broad armor + oversized conventional cannon

Flak Tank

flak_tank · Enclosed tracks · Quad autocannons

Uses your requested four-barrel design. The current procedural model has two barrels; this is an art direction choice for the replacement.

Create a low-poly tracked anti-aircraft tank with four autocannon barrels.

SILHOUETTE:
A low armored hull carrying a compact central turret and two paired gun
assemblies, one on each side. Exactly four barrels total:
two on the left and two on the right.
The guns point upward approximately 30 degrees in the presentation pose.

BODY:
Muted military green armor, charcoal gun assemblies,
and mustard-yellow identification panels.
Use a compact angular turret with a small central targeting sensor.
Keep the turret narrower than the hull so the paired guns stand out.

WEAPON:
Four thick, moderately long autocannon barrels with clear dark muzzle
openings. Use simple reinforced barrel sleeves and substantial receivers.
Each pair sits on a sturdy elevation cradle.
The weapon should resemble four separate autocannons, not one rotary gun.
Maintain visible spacing between every barrel.

RUNNING GEAR:
Tracked chassis with enclosed road wheels and broad side skirts.
Show only a simplified lower tread band.
Avoid exposed suspension and individual track links.

CHARACTER:
A squat anti-aircraft platform with a forked, upward-reaching silhouette.
The four raised barrels must distinguish it immediately from the Siege Tank
and Gatling Heavy, including in a small top-down thumbnail.

Suggested hull length: approximately 7 meters.
No missiles, giant central cannon, oversized radar dish, or firing effects.
Four raised barrels in two pairs

Missile Carrier

missile_carrier · Enclosed tracks · Box launcher
Create a low-poly armored tracked missile carrier with a large rectangular
launcher box.

SILHOUETTE:
A long low chassis with one broad slab-like launcher mounted above the rear
half. The launcher has two large rectangular missile channels side by side.
Its shape must read as a broad armored box rather than a cluster of tubes.

BODY:
Muted teal-green armor, charcoal chassis and launcher recesses,
pale ivory missile noses, and small burnt-orange warning panels.
Use a simple sloped front hull and heavy side skirts.
Keep the forward deck low so the rear launcher dominates the silhouette.

WEAPON:
One chunky rectangular launcher housing on a rotating base and substantial
elevation hinge. Pose the housing upward approximately 30 degrees.
Show two clearly separated launch channels with recessed pale missile noses.
Use thick armored edges and a compact rear counterweight.
Keep the rotating base, fixed cradle, and elevating launcher distinct.

RUNNING GEAR:
Enclosed tracked chassis with hidden road wheels.
Simple dark lower tread bands and broad armor skirts.
No detailed suspension or individually modeled track links.

CHARACTER:
A heavy mobile missile battery. Its main recognition feature is a wide,
tilted rectangular launcher. Keep it clearly different from the SAM
Launcher's four cylindrical canisters and radar dish.

Suggested hull length: approximately 7 meters.
No cylindrical tube cluster, large radar dish, secondary gun, or effects.
Broad launcher box + two channels

Gatling Heavy

gatling_heavy · Enclosed tracks · Rotary gun
Create a low-poly heavy tracked vehicle carrying one oversized rotary gun.

SILHOUETTE:
A broad squat armored chassis, compact angular turret,
and one thick cylindrical six-barrel gatling assembly.
The weapon should look like a huge rotating mechanical drum rather than
a conventional cannon.

BODY:
Muted slate-purple armor, charcoal weapon mechanics,
and restrained mustard-yellow recognition panels.
Give the turret a substantial rear ammunition housing.
Add one large simple ammunition feed enclosure connecting to the receiver;
avoid a chain made from individual bullets.

WEAPON:
Exactly six faceted barrels arranged around a central rotation axis.
Use a thick rear receiver, one supporting collar near the front,
and six visible dark muzzle openings.
Keep the barrel cluster short and chunky enough to remain legible at
gameplay distance. Give the assembly a clear longitudinal spin axis.
Separate the rotating barrel cluster from the stationary receiver and cradle.

RUNNING GEAR:
Enclosed tracked chassis with deep armor skirts.
Hide road wheels and simplify tracks into dark continuous lower bands.

CHARACTER:
A compact wall of sustained firepower. Slightly ridiculous weapon size,
with the circular barrel cluster as its unmistakable signature.

Suggested hull length: approximately 7 meters.
No extra cannons, missile pods, individual ammunition belts,
muzzle flashes, or spinning motion blur.
Six-barrel cluster + ammunition housing

Incendiary / Flame Tank

flame_tank · Enclosed tracks · Flame projector
Create a low-poly tracked flamethrower tank with two large rear fuel drums.

SILHOUETTE:
A squat armored hull, small forward turret, short thick flame projector,
and two oversized vertical fuel cylinders mounted side by side at the rear.
The twin rear cylinders should remain clearly visible from above.

BODY:
Dark olive armor, charcoal running gear, brick-red fuel drums,
and restrained orange-yellow warning panels.
Use thick protective plates around the lower halves of the drums,
while keeping their cylindrical upper shapes visible.
Add a broad rear heat shield and a few large painted vent shapes.

WEAPON:
A short, thick flame projector with a wide dark nozzle and angular
protective shroud. It should look industrial and heat-resistant.
Include one small recessed pilot-light detail.
Use a compact turret with a substantial elevation hinge.
Avoid the long slender barrel of a conventional tank gun.

RUNNING GEAR:
Enclosed tracks with road wheels hidden behind broad armor skirts.
Show only simple dark lower tread bands.

CHARACTER:
A dangerous little industrial furnace on tracks.
The short nozzle and twin red rear drums identify its role.
Keep the design chunky and readable rather than covered in pipes.

Suggested chassis length: approximately 6.2 meters.
No modeled flames, smoke, burning damage, loose hoses, crew,
or transparent fuel tanks.
Short nozzle + twin red fuel drums
10

Rail Crawler

rail_crawler · Enclosed tracks · Twin-rail weapon
Create a low-poly armored tracked railgun vehicle.

SILHOUETTE:
A low rectangular armored chassis carrying one exceptionally long,
straight railgun. The weapon has two parallel rectangular rails separated
by a clearly visible dark central gap.
Use a substantial rear breech to balance the long weapon.

BODY:
Muted blue-gray armor, charcoal gun structure,
and pale cyan energy inserts.
Use broad angular plates, a low turret, and two chunky rectangular
capacitor blocks mounted symmetrically on the rear deck.
Keep the body restrained so the long twin-rail weapon dominates.

WEAPON:
Two thick parallel rectangular rails held by a few broad structural braces.
Keep a visible separation between the rails along most of their length.
Add narrow cyan inset strips to the inner rail faces and a large squared
breech housing. Use solid colored inserts that remain readable without glow.
The muzzle is an open rectangular channel, not a round cannon bore.
Separate turret shell, elevation cradle, and complete rail assembly.

RUNNING GEAR:
Enclosed tracked chassis with hidden road wheels and broad armor skirts.
Use simplified dark lower tread bands.

CHARACTER:
Precise, heavy experimental artillery with an exaggerated straight-line
silhouette. More technological than the Siege Tank, while using the same
simple low-poly construction language.

Suggested chassis length: approximately 6.2 meters, excluding weapon overhang.
No floating components, energy beams, lightning, round cannon barrel,
missile pods, or oversized glowing effects.
Long open twin rails + cyan inserts
