Extraction with a clear flight plan.

World landing zones, recovered personnel, destructible installations and a moving escort transport now connect to the live mission system. The mission panel, radar diamonds and directional marker guide each phase.

How to extract a team
  1. Follow the amber pickup marker.
  2. Hover within 12 metres horizontally, 4–24 metres above the pad, at less than 7 m/s. Hold steady while the boarding bar fills.
  3. Follow the teal delivery marker. Stabilize again to unload and receive the mission reward.
  4. Collect the physical XP cache. Credits and salvage settle once on successful delivery.

Four extraction contracts

RESCUE / AVAILABLE FROM WAVE 1

Downed crew recovery

Find the marked crash pad, recover two crew members, and return them to a secure LZ. Four seconds to board, three to deliver; a 180-second window.

MEDICAL / AVAILABLE FROM WAVE 1

MEDEVAC

Recover three wounded personnel and deliver them to medical evacuation. Four seconds to board and three to unload; a 150-second window.

PRIORITY / AVAILABLE FROM WAVE 2

VIP evacuation

Collect a single priority officer and reach the marked evacuation site. Three seconds to board, four to transfer; a 130-second window.

INTELLIGENCE / AVAILABLE FROM WAVE 1

Black-box recovery

Retrieve the flight recorder from a wreck and deliver it to command. Three-second recovery and handover holds; a 160-second window.

END SORTIE / UNLOCKED AT WAVE 3

Tactical LZ extraction

Use Request Sortie Extraction in the mission panel. During an active task, the button explicitly offers Abort Task / Request Exfil. Fly to the new LZ and hold a steady hover for five seconds. Then press Confirm End Sortie / Extract to bank and leave through the normal results flow. Leaving the hover envelope removes readiness. The existing boss extraction choice remains available.
