Fly, aim, fight and extract.
Gamepad controls throughout the run.

Gamepad input now covers the operations center, aircraft and biome selection, hangar, settings, combat, upgrade cards, boss decisions, tactical extraction, debrief and retry. Keyboard and mouse controls remain available, with automatic switching when you deliberately use either device.

Start here: connect your gamepad, press a button, and use the left stick or D-pad to navigate. Press A to choose an item. In combat, the left stick moves the helicopter directly and the right stick points toward enemies for automatic target selection. The gun, HUD and salvo painter share the assisted aim point.

Menus and aircraft preview

Use the D-pad or left stick to move focus, A to confirm, and B to go back. B also dismisses the flight manual and confirmation dialogs. Upgrade cards have explicit left/right focus links; choose with A. Boss extraction/overdrive and debrief retry use the same controls.

On aircraft selection, the right stick rotates the preview, RT zooms in, and LT zooms out. The sortie dropdown and launch button are reachable by D-pad navigation.

Calibrate your right stick

Open Settings → Flight controls. Free-aim reach defaults to 1.00x and supports 0.30–2.50x. The radial deadzone defaults to 18% and supports 8–40%; increase it if the reticle drifts while the stick is released. Invert vertical aim if preferred. Preferences save with the existing settings file.

A radial deadzone rejects drift. Aim direction is anchored to the helicopter as the camera moves; targets do not require precise cursor placement. Without a valid target you can free-aim in that direction. The reticle stays in view and the real mouse pointer is not warped. Enable or disable hit rumble under Flight controls; hull and shield hits produce short pulses on the active controller.

Mission actions and safe extraction

Press View to open tactical operations. Combat pauses while you choose. The panel can cycle radar zoom, return to flight, or request an extraction LZ from wave 3. Requesting exfil asks for confirmation because it replaces the current task.

Ending the sortie is a separate confirmation after you reach and secure the marked LZ. The landing-zone hover requirements remain in force. A single press from regular combat cannot immediately end a run. Return with B, View or Start to continue the current flight.

Disconnects and input safety

  • Disconnecting the active controller during a sortie pauses the game and clears held controller actions. Reconnect and press Start to resume, or continue using keyboard and mouse.
  • Returning from a menu waits for held fire/salvo/action buttons to release. Confirming a menu choice cannot leak a shot into combat.
  • Pausing, opening cards or changing to a non-combat state cancels an in-progress salvo paint. A trigger released during a menu cannot fire stale locks afterward.
  • Mouse movement or a keyboard/mouse press restores mouse aiming; a deliberate gamepad input restores controller aiming.
  • The super ability's protection is now an owned damage-protection state. Its expiry preserves independent shields.
