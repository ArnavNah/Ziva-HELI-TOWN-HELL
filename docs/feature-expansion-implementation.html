The earlier mission/extraction pass remains active. This pass implements the remaining combat and progression systems, adds selectable desert and harbor districts, and prepares the audio pipeline for your supplied recordings.

How to play: choose the district in the dropdown immediately above LAUNCH SORTIE on aircraft selection. Town keeps the Heavy Gunship at wave 10; Desert Canyon uses the Mega Crawler; Harbor Night uses the Airborne Titan. Each run retains the same seven active ground vehicles.

Survival and combat feedback

  • A combat burst beginning above 30% hull cannot immediately kill the helicopter. A qualifying lethal hit leaves 1 HP and grants 0.5 seconds of grace. The burst snapshot lasts 0.2 seconds. Protection rearms only after 15 seconds and recovery to at least 60% hull.
  • Crashes and fuel starvation cannot trigger mortal protection. Active grace is an owned protection reason, so dash expiry cannot clear it.
  • Emergency siren, brief desaturation, and a readable hull alert; reduced red overlay and reduced-flash support preserve visibility.
  • Live homing missiles targeting the player get screen-edge warnings and warning tones. Urgency increases inside 25m or at less than one second estimated arrival. Existing railgun tracking telegraphs and directional damage arcs remain active.
  • Flare diversion now searches the actual projectile registry and ignores missiles targeting somebody else.
  • Explosions obey projectile target masks: player weapons cannot splash their own helicopter; enemy explosions do not wipe out their own cohort. Explosive damage can reach destructible world geometry. Existing single-scale damage and shield ownership fixes remain covered.
  • Restrained cannon camera kick affects the camera only and obeys the shake setting.

More tactical events

Rooftop supply: from wave 2, secure a real, level building roof. Clear hostiles within 30m and hold a stable hover for 8 seconds. The cache produces physical shield and overdrive pickups plus physical XP; it does not inject a free level-up draft. Losing the roof cancels the event.

SAM strike: the existing 45-second battery objective now reserves a heavy-attack slot, warns for 1.5 seconds, and fires a real, low-damage flak projectile each cycle. Destroying/cancelling the installation stops its attack cycle and releases its slot.

Several extraction missions, convoy escort, cargo delivery, marked targets, and explicit end-sortie confirmation remain in the mission and extraction guide.
